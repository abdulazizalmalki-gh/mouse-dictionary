License information for the bundled dictionary data
===================================================

The extension code remains MIT-licensed (see LICENSE). The dictionary packs
under data/ are separate data works with their own licenses:

data/dict/*.json5  (existing default pack, EN->JA)
  Based on ejdict-hand (https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict), Public Domain.

data/dict-en-ar/*.json5  (EN->AR pack)
  Derived from kaikki.org / Wiktextract machine-readable data extracted from
  English Wiktionary (https://kaikki.org, https://en.wiktionary.org).
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  Arabic definitions shown in parentheses are extracted from the Arabic
  Wiktionary (ar.wiktionary.org) dump, also CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikimedia
  Foundation). Arabic example sentences are drawn from Tatoeba
  (https://tatoeba.org), used under CC BY 2.0 FR; author attribution is
  available per sentence id (https://tatoeba.org/eng/sentences/show/<id>).
  Attribution: "This data includes content from English Wiktionary and
  Arabic Wiktionary, extracted by kaikki.org (Wiktextract) / Wikimedia,
  used under CC BY-SA 4.0, and example sentences from Tatoeba
  (CC BY 2.0 FR)."

data/dict-ja-ar/*.json5  (JA->AR pack)
  Derived by bridging two CC BY-SA 4.0 sources:
  1. JMdict/EDICT (https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), the
     Japanese-English dictionary of the Electronic Dictionary Research and
     Development Group, used under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the Japanese and English
     components; see https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html). This pack
     uses the jmdict-simplified release (https://github.com/scriptin/
     jmdict-simplified, same data, same license).
  2. The EN->AR pack above (kaikki.org / English Wiktionary, CC BY-SA 4.0)
     supplies the Arabic equivalents for English glosses.
  Attribution: "This data includes JMdict dictionary data from the Electronic
  Dictionary Research and Development Group, used under CC BY-SA 4.0, and
  content from English Wiktionary extracted by kaikki.org (Wiktextract), used
  under CC BY-SA 4.0."
  Example sentences are included from the jmdict-simplified examples release;
  those sentences are drawn from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), used under
  CC BY 2.0 FR. Each example is tagged in the source data with its Tatoeba
  sentence id, through which author attribution is available
  (https://tatoeba.org/eng/sentences/show/<id>).

ShareAlike note: the derived JA->AR mapping is published in this repository
under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms as its sources.

Build provenance: the packs in data/ are generated from upstream releases
(kaikki.org raw JSONL dump and jmdict-simplified 3.6.2) by the scripts in
tools/arabic-data/; no manual editing.
